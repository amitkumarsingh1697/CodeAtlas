# Seed Fundraising Data Room Checklist

## Corporate
- Incorporation and constitutional documents.
- Cap table, share issuances and option pool.
- Founder and employee agreements.
- Board/shareholder approvals.
- IP assignment and invention records.

## Product and technology
- Product demo and architecture.
- PRD/TDD and roadmap.
- Repository and release history.
- Security threat model and data flows.
- SBOM and open-source compliance report.
- Accuracy benchmark and methodology.
- Reliability/performance evidence.
- Known limitations and incident history.

## Customers and traction
- Signed contracts and SOWs.
- Invoices and collections.
- Customer references and permission.
- Case studies with measured outcomes.
- Pipeline with stage definitions.
- Retention, repeat purchase and expansion evidence.
- Customer concentration and churn.

## Financial
- Historical P&L and cash flow.
- Bank statements and tax records as appropriate.
- Monthly operating model.
- Revenue by stream and geography.
- Gross margin and delivery cost.
- Burn and runway.
- Forecast assumptions and sensitivity.
- Use of funds and hiring plan.

## Legal and compliance
- Trademark/domain status.
- Customer contracts and NDAs.
- Privacy/security policies.
- Open-source licence review.
- IP ownership.
- Litigation/disputes disclosure.
- Regulatory/tax advice.

## Investor materials
- Pitch deck.
- One-page overview.
- Product demo.
- Financial model.
- Cap table.
- Use-of-funds plan.
- Competitive landscape and differentiation evidence.
- Founder/team biographies with verified claims.
