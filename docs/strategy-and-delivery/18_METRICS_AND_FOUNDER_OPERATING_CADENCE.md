# Metrics & Founder Operating Cadence

## Weekly founder review
1. New target accounts added.
2. Outreach sent and replies.
3. Discovery calls completed.
4. Qualified opportunities.
5. Proposals sent and value.
6. Paid pilots won/lost and reasons.
7. Delivery status and founder hours.
8. Customer findings used and corrections.
9. Product defects and benchmark movement.
10. Cash balance, burn and runway.
11. Top three risks and decisions required.

## Product metrics
- Scan completion rate.
- Partial/failure rate.
- Time to first useful finding.
- Files scanned/supported/unsupported.
- Relationships by evidence class.
- Precision/recall by relationship type.
- Report generation success.
- Crash rate and resource usage.
- Repeat analysis and comparison use.
- Feature adoption by paying customers.

## Commercial metrics
- Qualified pipeline value.
- Proposal win rate.
- Sales cycle.
- Average contract value.
- Assessment delivery hours.
- Gross margin by offer.
- Assessment-to-recurring conversion.
- Renewal and expansion.
- Customer acquisition cost when measurable.

## Financial metrics
- Monthly recurring revenue (MRR).
- Annual recurring revenue (ARR).
- Services revenue separately.
- Gross profit and margin.
- Operating expenses.
- Net burn.
- Cash runway.
- Receivables and collection days.
- Revenue concentration.

## Cadence
- Monday: pipeline and priorities.
- Wednesday: product/accuracy/security checkpoint.
- Friday: sprint evidence, cash and customer learning review.
- Monthly: pricing, ICP and financial assumptions.
- Quarterly: strategy and investment readiness.
