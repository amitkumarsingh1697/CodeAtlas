# CodeAtlas — Company & Product Strategy

## 1. Executive thesis
CodeAtlas is a local-first software architecture intelligence platform. It analyzes existing repositories and produces an evidence-backed model of software structure, dependencies, data relationships, coverage and potential change impact. It is designed for engineering organizations and consultancies that need to understand unfamiliar systems without transferring source code to a third-party SaaS service.

**Positioning:** Local-first Software Architecture Intelligence.  
**Promise:** Understand what exists, see how it connects, and identify what may be affected before making changes.

## 2. Problem
Organizations inherit codebases that are poorly documented, complex, old, distributed across languages and frameworks, or unfamiliar to the team. Manual discovery is expensive and slow. Existing diagrams and documentation may be stale. Static analysis and AI-generated explanations can be incomplete or misleading unless evidence and uncertainty are visible.

## 3. Initial wedge
Sell a fixed-scope **CodeAtlas Architecture Intelligence Assessment** to boutique and mid-sized modernization consultancies and software engineering firms. The initial buyer is typically a founder/CEO, CTO, delivery head, modernization director or engineering practice leader.

Target account hypotheses:
- Approximately 30–500 engineers (not a hard exclusion).
- Repeatedly inherit or assess client repositories.
- Perform modernization, migration, maintenance, due diligence or technical discovery.
- Have confidentiality constraints that make source upload to SaaS unattractive.
- Can approve a bounded pilot without lengthy enterprise procurement.

## 4. Product stages
### Stage 1 — Assessment
Customer-operated local analysis; fixed scope; professional technical and executive report; findings presentation; explicit coverage and limitations.

### Stage 2 — Professional
Paid individual/team workflows: repeatable analysis, report generation, comparison, advanced impact analysis, project workspaces and supported integrations. Exact packaging must be validated with customers.

### Stage 3 — Enterprise
Organization administration, policy controls, role-based access, audit, multiple repositories, CI integration, architecture baselines/drift, private deployment and support.

## 5. Differentiation
Do not claim that local execution alone is unique. The intended combined differentiation is:
1. Transparent relationship labels: observed, inferred, unknown.
2. Evidence references to file/line and explainable provenance.
3. Explicit analysis coverage, exclusions and unresolved findings.
4. Cross-layer connections across source, API, configuration, database and supported infrastructure artifacts.
5. Local-first operation with clear data-flow guarantees.
6. Consultancy-ready assessment and repeatable delivery workflow.
7. Stack-specific published accuracy benchmarks.

## 6. Product principles
- Evidence over confident-sounding guesses.
- Unknown is an acceptable and useful result.
- No false completeness claims.
- Core static analysis works without external LLM calls.
- AI enrichment is optional, isolated and disclosed.
- Customer controls source, output and retention.
- Every capability has a defined support level and test evidence.
- Build customer outcomes before visual novelty.
- Support both selected stacks, but do not claim equal maturity.

## 7. Market approach
Run India and international validation in parallel.
- India: boutique consultancies, specialist engineering firms, modernization practices.
- International: boutique modernization and legacy transformation consultancies in US, UK, Europe and Australia.
Avoid relying initially on very large IT services procurement processes.

## 8. Business model
Initial fixed-scope assessment revenue, followed by annual or monthly Professional and Enterprise licences. Services are a customer-discovery and onboarding mechanism, not the desired long-term revenue dependency.

## 9. North-star business question
How many customers pay for an assessment, trust its findings, and then want CodeAtlas to remain in their engineering workflow?

## 10. Strategic non-goals for the first 90 days
- Broad self-serve SaaS.
- Equal-depth support for every language.
- Full AI coding agent platform.
- SOC 2 or ISO claims without completed independent assurance.
- Advanced 3D polish unless it improves comprehension or conversion.
- Large sales team or fundraising before evidence of repeatable demand.

## 11. Brand note
CodeAtlas is a working product name pending trademark, domain, company-name and category-conflict clearance. Do not commit to major brand spend or public launch until counsel and a structured clearance search have been completed. ArchLens remains the visualization/exploration experience, subject to name clearance too.
