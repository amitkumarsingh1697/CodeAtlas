# Financial Model — Assumptions & Instructions

The accompanying spreadsheet is an editable scenario model, not a forecast or valuation. All assumptions must be replaced with actual quotes, payroll, tax and customer data.

## Revenue streams
1. Architecture assessment revenue.
2. Professional subscriptions.
3. Enterprise annual contracts.
4. Separately scoped services/custom deployment (track separately to avoid masking software margins).

## Cost categories
- Founder and employee compensation.
- Contractors and specialist engineering.
- Legal/IP/trademark.
- Security testing and audits.
- Cloud, CI, software and developer tooling.
- Travel, events and sales.
- Payment processing, FX and tax/accounting.
- Insurance and support.

## Model principles
- Separate bookings, recognized revenue, cash receipts and deferred revenue.
- Show India and international revenue separately.
- Track GST/VAT/withholding and FX with professional advice.
- Include payment delay and bad-debt assumptions.
- Separate assessment delivery cost from product R&D.
- Calculate gross margin by stream.
- Model hiring only when revenue/funding supports it.
- Do not use speculative TAM as a revenue forecast.
- Add downside/base/upside cases.
- Include monthly cash runway and funding requirement.
