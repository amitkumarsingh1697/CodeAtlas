# CodeAtlas — Pricing & Packaging Strategy

All prices are **hypotheses** to validate through paid proposals. They are not published commitments or market facts.

## 1. Initial offer: Architecture Intelligence Assessment
### India
Test ₹1.5 lakh, ₹2.25 lakh and ₹3 lakh tiers based on scope and complexity.

### International
Test USD 5,000, 6,000 and 7,500 tiers.

### Scope variables
- One repository or monorepo.
- Approximate source size and build complexity.
- Supported language/framework coverage.
- Number of database schemas.
- Number of services/modules.
- Deployment and security constraints.
- Required report depth and workshop time.
- Turnaround and support.

Do not price solely by LOC. Use a scoping call and written exclusions.

## 2. Proposed assessment packages
| Package | India test price | Global test price | Scope |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Discovery | ₹1.5L | $5,000 | One bounded repository, inventory, architecture overview, coverage and findings |
| Standard | ₹2.25L | $6,000 | Discovery plus dependency, API/database findings and impact walkthrough |
| Complex | ₹3L+ | $7,500+ | Larger/multi-module scope, deeper framework review and workshop |

These are starting experiments. Quote custom scope only after discovery.

## 3. Software tiers
### Community
Free/open-source core. Individual local analysis, basic exploration and community support. Exact feature boundary depends on legal and engineering design.

### Professional
Test pricing only after recurring value is proven. Potential unit: per developer seat, per workstation, or per organization. Avoid deciding the unit before buyer research. Include advanced reports, repeat runs, comparisons and professional support if validated.

### Enterprise
Annual contract, scoped by deployment, repository count, users, support, governance and integrations. Do not publish an enterprise price until customer discovery and cost-to-serve data exist.

## 4. Commercial principles
- Annual prepayment discount should be modest and explicit.
- Paid pilot fee can be credited partly toward annual licence only if commercially justified.
- Charge separately for custom integrations and unsupported stacks.
- No unlimited repositories or unlimited consulting in entry plans.
- Define report revisions and support window.
- Quote currency, taxes, payment terms and validity.
- For international contracts, address FX, withholding tax, VAT/GST, governing law and payment fees with professional advice.

## 5. Willingness-to-pay experiments
Run structured proposals at different price points with comparable scope. Track:
- accepted/rejected;
- reason;
- budget owner;
- procurement cycle;
- perceived value;
- alternatives and current spend;
- scope changes requested.
Do not infer willingness to pay from compliments or free-trial usage.
