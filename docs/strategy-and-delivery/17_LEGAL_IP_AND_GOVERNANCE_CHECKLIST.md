# Legal, IP & Governance Checklist

This is a checklist for professional legal/accounting advice, not legal advice.

## Company and ownership
- Confirm entity structure and founder equity.
- Founder IP assignment to company.
- Employee/contractor invention assignment and confidentiality.
- Invention and third-party contribution records.
- Board/shareholder approvals where required.

## Open source
- Inventory all dependencies and licences.
- Preserve Apache-2.0 notices and attribution.
- Review patents, trademarks and NOTICE obligations.
- Define contribution policy and CLA/DCO approach with counsel.
- Separate proprietary modules without copying incompatible code.
- Document compatibility between open and commercial components.
- Review source availability and distribution obligations.

## Brand
- Trademark clearance in relevant classes and jurisdictions.
- Domain and social handle review.
- Company and product name conflicts.
- Review CodeAtlas and ArchLens before public investment.

## Customer contracts
- NDA.
- Master services agreement.
- Statement of work.
- Software licence/EULA.
- Data processing terms where applicable.
- Confidentiality and customer data ownership.
- Security responsibilities.
- IP ownership and feedback rights.
- Liability, indemnity and warranty provisions.
- Termination and deletion.
- Governing law and dispute resolution.

## Privacy and security
- Data inventory and data-flow map.
- Privacy notice for any personal data.
- Retention and deletion policy.
- Incident response and breach notification process.
- Vulnerability disclosure policy.
- Vendor/subprocessor inventory.
- International data transfer review where applicable.

## India and international operations
- GST, invoicing and export-of-services review.
- Foreign exchange and payment collection rules.
- Withholding tax and tax treaty advice.
- VAT/sales tax exposure.
- Employment and contractor classification.
- Country-specific privacy and contract requirements.

## Fundraising
- Cap table and fully diluted ownership.
- Founder vesting and IP assignment.
- Financial statements and tax filings.
- Material contracts and liabilities.
- Open-source compliance report.
- Customer consent for logos/case studies.
- Option pool and financing documents.
